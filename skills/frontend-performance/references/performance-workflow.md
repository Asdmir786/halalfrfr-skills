# Performance Workflow

## 1. Observe

What feels slow or unstable?

## 2. Reproduce

Create a reliable path to the issue.

## 3. Measure

Use appropriate lab or field tools.

## 4. Identify

Find the expensive resource, task, render, layout, or interaction.

## 5. Hypothesize

Explain why the issue happens.

## 6. Change

Apply the smallest change likely to solve the cause.

## 7. Measure Again

Confirm improvement.

## 8. Regression Review

Verify:

- visuals
- interactions
- responsive behavior
- accessibility
- business functionality

## Principle

A performance change without measurement is a hypothesis, not a confirmed optimization.
