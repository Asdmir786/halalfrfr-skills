# Anti-Generic Design

The goal is not to ban familiar patterns.

The goal is to prevent automatic design.

## Common Automatic Patterns

Watch for repeated defaults such as:

- centered headline
- short subheading
- two buttons
- gradient glow
- floating dashboard
- logo strip
- three cards
- bento grid
- testimonial cards
- pricing cards
- FAQ
- generic footer

These structures may be correct.

They become weak when chosen without considering the product.

## Common AI Visual Habits

Watch for:

- purple-blue gradients without brand reason
- excessive glowing borders
- too many glass surfaces
- nested cards
- unnecessary pills
- rounded rectangles everywhere
- decorative grid backgrounds
- arbitrary floating particles
- generic abstract blobs
- repeated icon-plus-heading cards
- identical section heights
- identical animation timing
- excessive entrance animation
- random 3D objects
- generic fake dashboards
- excessive blur

## Replacement Question

Instead of asking:

How can this section look cooler?

Ask:

What should this section communicate, and what composition communicates that most strongly?

## Specificity

Prefer product-specific visual material.

Examples:

generic floating cube
versus
actual product object

generic analytics card
versus
real product workflow

random gradient
versus
brand-derived color field

decorative animation
versus
motion that explains transformation

## Originality

Originality can come from:

- unusual scale
- specific photography
- typography
- composition
- sequencing
- interaction
- product detail
- material treatment
- narrative

It does not require visual chaos.

## Consistency

A distinctive visual technique becomes part of the identity only when it is repeated with discipline.

Do not invent a new design language for every section.

## Restraint

Premium work often depends on editing.

Remove:

- effects with no job
- repeated decoration
- weak secondary CTAs
- redundant badges
- unnecessary card containers
- competing focal points

## Final Test

If the interface could be swapped into an unrelated AI startup with only a logo and text change, it is probably not specific enough.
