# Redesign Preservation Contract

Before redesign, identify what must not break.

## Functionality

Protect:

- navigation
- forms
- actions
- state
- data
- routing

## Integrations

Protect:

- analytics hooks
- API wiring
- auth
- permissions
- external widgets
- business workflows

## Architecture

Do not replace working architecture unless the redesign requires structural change.

## Accessibility

Preserve accessible semantics and interaction.

## Content

Do not replace real content with invented marketing copy unless requested.

## Visual Identity

Preserve recognizable brand elements unless rebranding is part of the request.

## Principle

A successful redesign improves the experience without silently changing the product.
