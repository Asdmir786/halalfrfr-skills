# Surface and Typography Polish

## Surface Hierarchy

Use the minimum number of visible layers needed to explain grouping.

Possible hierarchy:

page
section
surface
elevated surface
overlay

Not every level must have a different background.

## Borders

Use consistent roles such as:

- subtle separation
- standard control border
- emphasized interactive border

## Shadows

Use shadows according to elevation.

Avoid unrelated shadow recipes across similar components.

## Typography

Review:

- hierarchy
- line height
- line length
- weight
- tracking
- muted contrast
- numeric behavior

## Muted Text

Muted does not mean unreadable.

Secondary text still needs sufficient clarity.

## Labels

Labels should be concise and visually consistent.

## Dense Interfaces

In dense interfaces, small spacing inconsistencies become especially visible.

Prioritize alignment and predictable rhythm over excessive whitespace.

## Marketing Interfaces

In marketing interfaces, allow scale and whitespace to carry more of the hierarchy.

Do not convert every text group into a card.
