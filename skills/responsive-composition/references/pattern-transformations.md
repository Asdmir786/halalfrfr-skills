# Responsive Pattern Transformations

These are options, not mandatory templates.

## Two-Column Marketing Section

Wide:
text and media side by side

Narrow:
media above or below text according to narrative priority

## Sidebar Application

Wide:
persistent sidebar

Intermediate:
compact rail

Narrow:
drawer or sheet

## Toolbar

Wide:
full actions

Narrow:
primary actions remain visible, secondary actions move to overflow

## Table

Wide:
full comparison

Narrow:
horizontal scroll, reduced secondary columns, or focused detail flow

Choose based on the table's purpose.

## Hero

Wide:
large composition with supporting visual layers

Narrow:
reduce layers, protect primary message, preserve one strong visual anchor

## Horizontal Storytelling

Wide:
horizontal or pinned sequence

Narrow:
vertical narrative sequence

## Multi-Panel Cards

Wide:
grid

Narrow:
stack, horizontal scroller, or prioritized sequence

Do not choose a carousel merely to avoid designing the mobile composition.

## Complex Background

Wide:
full effect

Narrow:
simplified effect or static equivalent when needed for clarity and performance.
