# Asset Decision Matrix

Choose the simplest medium that communicates the idea strongly.

## CSS

Best for:

- surfaces
- layout
- simple gradients
- borders
- shadows
- basic geometric decoration

## SVG

Best for:

- icons
- diagrams
- simple illustrations
- scalable geometric graphics

## Raster Image

Best for:

- photography
- detailed illustration
- product renders
- textures
- realistic scenes

## Transparent Raster

Best for:

- isolated products
- layered compositions
- foreground objects
- controlled overlap

## Video

Best for:

- cinematic motion
- rendered transformation
- complex animation that does not require interaction

## Image Sequence

Best for:

- controlled scroll-linked frame progression
- product rotations
- rendered transitions

Use carefully because of payload size.

## Shader

Best for:

- procedural backgrounds
- distortion
- fluid visual fields
- lightweight GPU-driven abstraction

## Real-Time 3D

Best for:

- interactive physical products
- spatial exploration
- simulation
- user-controlled camera or object interaction

Real-time 3D carries the highest implementation and performance complexity.

Use it when that complexity produces meaningful value.
